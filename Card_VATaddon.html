VAT addon

to convert anything (or almost) in Vertex Animation Texture

This is Still in developpement, the result of my master degree research. It will be available in the end of August. This C# addon for Unity will help you convert various animations into VATs for VFX or crowd sim purpose mostly. I aim to create a tool that is useful in multiple cases, with a research's work to benchmark various iterations.
