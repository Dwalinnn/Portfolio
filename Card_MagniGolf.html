MagniGolf

a golf game with magnets

MagniGolf is a small project during my master degree for school. Due to the incredible reslut '20/20', i had to mention it here ^^

This prototype was made in Unity, I mostly worked the User Interface, but i also helped with the code

The idea is to drag and drop magnets to influence the level then play like a casual minigolf game with the magnet twist

Since this exercice, i felt more confident about the Unity pipeline gameManager, UI, Sounddesign and .cs in general.
